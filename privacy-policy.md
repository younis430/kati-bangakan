# Privacy Policy — Kati Bangakan (کاتی بانگەکان)

**Last updated:** 6 October 2026 · **App package:** `com.younis.katibangakan`

## Summary

Kati Bangakan does not collect, store or share personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## Location

If you enable automatic location, the app reads your device location **on the device only** to determine your city for prayer times and the qibla direction. The location is never sent to us or to any third party and is not stored beyond the current session. You can instead choose a city manually; the app works fully without the location permission.

## Notifications and alarms

Notification, exact-alarm and full-screen permissions are used only to play the adhan and show reminders (adhkar, Quran khatm) at the times you configure. All schedules stay on your device.

## Internet use

The app connects to the internet only to download Quran audio, look up city names, and translate content into your selected language (via Google Translate). These requests contain only the text or coordinates needed for the request and no identifiers about you. Everything else — prayer times, Quran text, hadith, adhkar — is bundled and works offline.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
